<h3 align="center">tradoor terminal</h3>

<p align="center">
  A fast, open-source trading terminal in the browser.<br>
  Charts, order flow, market profile and your own indicators, drawn by a Rust engine in WebAssembly, live from fourteen exchanges.
</p>

- **Charts:** up to 20 at once, one minute to a year, drawings, bar replay, alerts to Telegram
- **Order flow:** DOM, tape, footprints, TPO profiles, liquidations and net taker flow
- **Frost Script:** indicators of your own, shared in the Community, safe by design
- **Journal and paper trading:** every position you draw is a trade, settled by price
- **Free and open:** AGPL-3.0, one Docker image to host it yourself
